Frequently Asked Questions

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your device only. Worthi never uploads your balances or account details to any server. There is no account registration and no cloud sync.

What happens if I delete the app?

All data is removed with the app. There is no backup or cloud copy. If you want to keep your data, export it first via Settings → Data → Download Template, or use Settings → Data → Import from CSV to back up and restore.

How do I add a credit card or loan?

Tap the + button on the home screen, choose Credit Card or Loan, then enter the amount you owe as a positive number. Worthi stores it as a negative value and shows it in red, so it reduces your net worth correctly.

How does the investment return calculation work?

When adding an investment entry, enter both the total deposited (cumulative running total of money you've put in) and the current value. Return is calculated as (current value − deposited) ÷ deposited × 100.

How does a shared house work?

When adding a property, toggle "Shared asset" and enter your ownership percentage. Worthi calculates your net worth contribution as your share of the equity (property value minus mortgage balance × your ownership %).

Why do archived accounts still appear in the graph?

Archived accounts are hidden from the account list but their historical data is preserved in the net worth graph. This keeps your history accurate. You can restore an archived account any time from Settings → Archived Accounts.

How do I import data from a spreadsheet?

Go to Settings → Data → Download Template to get a CSV with the correct column format. Fill it in with your accounts and entries, then use Settings → Data → Import from CSV to load it into the app.

Can I change the currency?

Yes — go to Settings and tap the currency row. Changing it relabels all values with the new symbol. No conversion is applied, so enter all values in your chosen currency.

Does Worthi collect any personal data?

No. Worthi uses anonymous analytics (Mixpanel) to understand how the app is used — no names, emails, or financial values are ever sent. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
